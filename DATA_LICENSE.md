`LICENSE`

## Analysis-ready data

The main analysis-ready dataset is:

`data/input/ureh_units.gpkg`

This dataset is an integrated research product derived from multiple spatial,
hydrological, remote-sensing, and cartographic sources together with
project-specific processing.

The repository does not redistribute the complete original raw datasets used
during the upstream geospatial preprocessing.

Some information incorporated into the analysis-ready dataset originates from
external data providers. Rights, attribution requirements, and terms of use
associated with those original sources remain with their respective providers.

This repository does not attempt to relicense third-party source data.

Users wishing to reuse the analysis-ready dataset beyond reproduction of the
associated study should verify the applicable terms of the original data
providers and provide appropriate attribution.

## Model specification

Files under:

`data/model/`

contain the operational Bayesian-network specification used in the study,
including state definitions, conditional probability tables, and provenance
information.

These files are provided to support transparency and computational
reproducibility of the published analysis.

Reuse should acknowledge this repository and the associated manuscript.

## Derived analytical outputs

Files under:

`results/tables/`

and:

`results/spatial/`

are derived analytical products generated by the reproducible workflow
implemented in this repository.

They are provided for scientific transparency, verification, and reuse with
appropriate citation of the repository and associated manuscript.

Where a derived product contains or incorporates information originating from
third-party datasets, the terms of the original provider may still apply to
those underlying components.

## Manuscript figures

Files under:

`results/figures/`

are publication-ready graphical products prepared for the associated
manuscript.

They are not covered by the MIT software license.

Reuse of manuscript figures should cite the associated article and repository
and must comply with any copyright or reuse conditions applied by the
publisher after publication.
