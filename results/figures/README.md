# Manuscript figures

This directory contains the final figures prepared for the manuscript on
Ecohydrological Response Units (UREH) in the Cuitzmala River Basin, Jalisco,
Mexico.

The numerical and spatial results underlying these figures are generated by
the reproducible analytical workflow implemented in `R/01`–`R/13`.

The corresponding reproducible outputs are available in:

- `results/tables/`
- `results/spatial/ureh_results.gpkg`

Final figure composition involved a combination of R, QGIS, and graphical
editing. Therefore, `run_all.R` reproduces the analytical and spatial results
underlying the figures, but does not reproduce their final pixel-identical
layout.

## Figures

- `Fig01_study_area.png` — Study area and spatial analysis units.
- `Fig02_bayesian_network.png` — Bayesian-network architecture used for UREH inference.
- `Fig03_UREH_posterior_support.png` — Dominant UREH regionalization and posterior classification margin.
- `Fig04_sensitivity_TM.png` — Sensitivity scenarios M1–M3 and Thornthwaite–Mather diagnostic.
- `Fig05_clustering_functional_composition.png` — C1–C2 comparison and functional/biophysical composition of C2 clusters.
